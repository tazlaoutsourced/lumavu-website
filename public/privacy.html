Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version. The Lumavu app doesn't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It connects to Apple and to whatever your playlists point to, and nothing comes to us. If you sync with iCloud, your playlists and lists are kept in your own iCloud account, which we can't see. This website keeps one thing: your email address, if you join the waitlist, so we can tell you when Lumavu comes out.

Who we are

Lumavu is made by Thinkers & Builders ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your data, email hello@thinkersandbuilders.co.uk.

The Lumavu app

Playlists, accounts and servers you add

When you add an Xtream Codes account, an M3U playlist, a Jellyfin or Emby server, or a guide, Lumavu stores its details on your device. Passwords are kept in your device's Keychain. Lumavu connects straight from your device to those servers to fetch channels, guides, artwork and video. We never receive these details, and we never see what you watch. The companies that run those servers have their own privacy policies, and they will see the connections your device makes to them, as with any app or website.

Free sample channels

If you choose to load the sample channels or the free news channels, Lumavu downloads public lists and guides from the community projects that publish them. Those services see an ordinary web request from your device. Nothing else is sent.

Live subtitles and translation

Live subtitles use Apple's speech recognition and translation, running on your device. The audio and the text it produces stay on your device. The first time you use a language, its language files are downloaded from Apple.

iCloud sync

Lumavu can keep your playlists (with their logins), favourites, lists, watch progress, hidden and locked categories, extra guides and settings the same on every iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV signed in to your iCloud account. It's on unless you turn it off in Lumavu's settings, under iCloud. This uses Apple's iCloud storage in your own account. Apple stores it under your Apple Account, and we can't see it or get at it. Downloads, recordings and playlists imported from a file aren't synced.

What stays on your device

  • Recordings, and downloaded films and episodes.
  • Playlists imported from a file.
  • Everything else listed under iCloud sync, if you've turned sync off.
  • The app's logs. They stay on your device unless you choose to share them with us.
  • Backups, which are saved as files wherever you choose.

Purchases

Lumavu Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment and never shares your payment details with us. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your device. Apple gives developers sales reports that don't identify you.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send us crash reports and usage figures that don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

This website

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store your email address and the date you joined. We use it for one thing: to email you when Lumavu is available on the App Store. We don't share it with anyone or use it for marketing. Once we've sent that email, we delete the list. We rely on your consent to keep it. You can withdraw it at any time: email us and we'll take you off.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. The waitlist is stored with Cloudflare too. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party trackers. Its fonts are served from the site itself.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. For the waitlist that's your email address and nothing more. Email hello@thinkersandbuilders.co.uk and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Lumavu doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children. Parents can lock categories behind a PIN in the app's settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Lumavu or this site handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
