Lumavu Pro

Lumavu is free to use with one playlist on each device, one favourites list and the guide from yesterday to tomorrow. Lumavu Pro adds sync across your devices, the whole week's guide with reminders and extra guides, Multiview, recordings, downloads, and as many playlists and lists as you like. Pro is available as:

  • A monthly or yearly subscription. It renews automatically at the end of each period unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the period ends. Payment is taken by Apple when you confirm the purchase and at each renewal.
  • A free trial. The yearly plan includes 7 days free for new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it becomes a paid yearly subscription.

One purchase works on all your Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Pro isn't shared through Family Sharing. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may change. A price change won't affect a period you've already paid for.
