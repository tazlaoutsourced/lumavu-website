Xtream Codes player for Apple TV

Updated 27 November 2026. Steps are for Lumavu on Apple TV; the same login works on iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

The short version. An Xtream Codes login is three things: a server address, a username and a password. On Apple TV, install a player that supports Xtream, such as Lumavu, choose Add playlist, then Xtream Codes, and type them in. With Lumavu Pro, add it once on your iPhone and it appears on your Apple TV through iCloud.

What an Xtream Codes login is

Xtream Codes is a common server system behind many playlists. Instead of a single link, you sign in, and the player gets live streams, films, series, the TV guide and catch-up as separate sections. The server address usually looks like http://example.com:8080.

If you only have an M3U link with username= and password= in it, that's an Xtream account too. Lumavu recognises it and signs in for you.

How to add Xtream Codes on Apple TV

  1. Install Lumavu on Apple TV. It's free on the App Store and needs tvOS 18 or later. Try Lumavu on the App Store.
  2. Add playlist, then Xtream Codes. Enter the server address, username and password. When Apple TV shows its keyboard, a nearby iPhone can offer to type for you.
  3. Give it a name and colour. It shows in the sidebar.
  4. Or skip typing. With Lumavu Pro, add the login on your iPhone, iPhone Duo or iPad and iCloud sync brings it to Apple TV, with your favourites and where you left off. Passwords are kept in the Keychain.

What you get on Apple TV

  • A TV guide from the account, and catch-up where the account offers it.
  • Films and series that remember where you stopped.
  • One entry for 4K, HD and SD copies, with automatic fallback if one fails.
  • Live subtitles written on the device (Apple TV writes them but doesn't translate them).
  • Multiview (Pro). Up to four at once, if your account allows that many connections.

What Lumavu doesn't do on Apple TV yet: Jellyfin or Emby live TV, and recording, which runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac while the app is open.

Other Xtream players for Apple TV

UHF, IPTVX, Smarters Player Lite and iPlayTV also sign in with Xtream on Apple TV. UHF runs back to tvOS 16, so it's the one to try if your Apple TV can't update to tvOS 18. See our comparison.

Questions

Can Apple TV play Xtream Codes?

Not by itself. Install a player that supports Xtream Codes, such as Lumavu, UHF or IPTVX, and sign in with the server address, username and password.

Which Apple TV models can run Lumavu?

Any Apple TV that runs tvOS 18 or later.

Do I need to type the login with the Apple TV remote?

No. You can use a nearby iPhone's keyboard, or, with Lumavu Pro, add the login on iPhone and let iCloud sync bring it to Apple TV.

Is Xtream Codes the same as M3U?

They often come from the same account. M3U is a single list; signing in as Xtream also brings films, series, the guide and catch-up as separate sections.

Does Lumavu include an Xtream account?

No. Lumavu is a player for a login you already have.
