Lumavu vs TiviMate

A comparison of two IPTV players. TiviMate facts are taken from tivimate.com. Lumavu facts are taken from the App Store listing and this site.

The short version. TiviMate is an IPTV player for Android TV. It is not available on iPhone, iPad or Apple TV. Lumavu is a player for the same kind of playlist — an M3U link or an Xtream Codes login — on those Apple devices. Neither app includes any channels. You bring the playlist or account you already have.

Does TiviMate run on Apple TV or iPhone?

No. TiviMate's own website says the app is intended for Android TV devices, is not optimised for phones and tablets, and is installed from Google Play or by sideloading an APK. There is no official TiviMate app on the App Store.

If you have arrived from a page offering "TiviMate for iOS", a TiviMate IPA, or TiviMate for Apple TV, that is not TiviMate. Those pages are often misleading.

What the two players are for

Both are players for a playlist or account you already have. TiviMate's site is clear that it doesn't provide any sources of TV channels. Lumavu works the same way: it is a player only, and does not sell or include any channels.

TiviMate is built around a television with a remote, on Android TV. Lumavu is built for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Apple TV, including the Siri Remote on the TV.

When Lumavu is a good fit

Lumavu is worth a look if you already use TiviMate on Android TV and want a player for the same playlist on an iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad or Apple TV, or if your screens are Apple to begin with. One Lumavu Pro purchase covers those Apple devices on the same Apple Account.

It also connects to a Jellyfin or Emby server for films and series. Live TV from those servers is not available in Lumavu yet.

On Android TV, TiviMate remains the official app. The two can sit side by side, each with the same playlist.

What Lumavu costs

Lumavu is free to use with one playlist on each device, one favourites list, and the guide from yesterday to tomorrow. Lumavu Pro adds sync across your devices, the whole week's guide, Multiview, recordings, downloads, and as many playlists as you like. In the UK, Pro is £2.99 a month, £19.99 a year (with seven days free for new subscribers) or £49.99 once. The App Store shows the price in your own currency.

Questions

Is there an official TiviMate app for Apple TV or iPhone?

No. TiviMate's website says the app is intended for Android TV and is installed from Google Play or as an APK. There is no official TiviMate app on the App Store.

Will my existing playlist work in Lumavu?

If you have an M3U playlist or an Xtream Codes login, add it in Lumavu the same way you would in any other player. Lumavu doesn't include any channels, and it doesn't receive your playlist: the details stay on your device.

Does Lumavu work on Android TV?

No. Lumavu is an Apple app, for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Apple TV. If you already use TiviMate on Android TV, you can keep it there and use Lumavu on your Apple devices.

Does Lumavu come with channels?

No. It is a player for the playlist or server you already have.
