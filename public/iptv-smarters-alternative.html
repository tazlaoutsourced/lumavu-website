An IPTV Smarters alternative for iPhone and Apple TV

Smarters Player Lite facts are taken from its US App Store listing by WHMCS SMARTERS. Lumavu facts are taken from the App Store listing and this site.

The short version. IPTV Smarters is a name many people are given when they first set up a player. On the App Store, the official app from WHMCS SMARTERS is called Smarters Player Lite, for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. Lumavu is a different player for the M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login you already have: no ads, no account, no tracking. Neither app includes any channels.

What Smarters Player Lite is

According to its App Store listing, Smarters Player Lite is a media player that supports M3U and JSON playlists, among other formats. It is free to download, with in-app purchases, and is listed for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. The listing states that it contains advertising, and the privacy label says usage data may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. It requires iOS 15.6 or later, and tvOS 12 or later.

The listing is also clear that it is a standalone player and does not provide, host or sell any media or TV channels.

Other apps use a similar name, which can make the App Store search confusing. This page compares Lumavu with the official Smarters Player Lite listing, not with every similarly named app.

Why people look for an alternative

People usually want a player that takes the same kind of playlist, without ads, and that feels comfortable on Apple TV as well as on the phone. Lumavu is built for that: no account, no ads, no tracking, and one app for iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Apple TV.

Lumavu does need a newer system version — iOS 18, iPadOS 18 or tvOS 18 or later. If your device cannot update that far, Smarters Player Lite may still run, because its listing goes back to iOS 15.6 and tvOS 12.

Moving your playlist

If you already have an M3U link, paste it into Lumavu. If you have an Xtream Codes server address, username and password, add those instead. Nothing has to be exported from another player. The details stay on your device, in the Keychain where a password is needed, and Lumavu does not receive them.

Lumavu Pro can then keep that playlist, your favourites and where you left off the same on every iPhone, iPhone Duo, iPad and Apple TV signed in to your iCloud account.

Questions

Is Lumavu the same as IPTV Smarters?

No. Lumavu is a separate app from Thinkers & Builders. It is not affiliated with IPTV Smarters or WHMCS SMARTERS.

Will the playlist I used in Smarters work in Lumavu?

If you have the M3U link or the Xtream Codes login, add it in Lumavu. Lumavu doesn't include any channels, and it doesn't need an export from another player.

Does Lumavu have ads?

No. There are no ads, no account and no tracking.

Does Lumavu come with channels?

No. It is a player for the playlist or server you already have.
