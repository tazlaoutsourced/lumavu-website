Bring what you already have

Sign in to an Xtream Codes account, paste an M3U link or open a playlist file, or point Lumavu at your own Jellyfin or Emby server. Give each one a colour and an icon so you can tell them apart, and decide how often it refreshes

When a stream drops, Lumavu finds another

Playlists often carry the same channel more than once. There might be a 4K copy, an HD one and an SD backup, and the same channel again in your second playlist. Lumavu treats them as one channel and starts on the copy you prefer

  4. ✓

Four channels at once. You pick the one you hear

Tap a tile to hear it, double-tap to make it full screen. Most accounts only allow one or two streams at a time, so Lumavu keeps count. It plays each channel from whichever playlist has a stream free, and starts a waiting tile as soon as one opens up

Subtitles for live TV, written as it's spoken

Turn them on and Lumavu listens to the channel and types out what's being said, two lines at a time like broadcast captions, so the words stay still while you read them. It can translate as it goes, with the original underneath in smaller type

NDNorthline Docs · The Empty QuarterLive subtitles

The listening and the translating both happen on your device, using Apple's own speech and translation tools, so nothing is sent anywhere. There are more than twenty languages, and each one downloads the first time you pick it. Live subtitles need iOS, iPadOS, macOS or tvOS 26. On Apple TV they're written out but not translated

Questions

More on the support page

Does Lumavu come with channels?

No, it's a player. You add your own IPTV playlists, Xtream Codes accounts or media servers. If you want a look around first, you can load some free sample films, series and news channels

What can I add?

Xtream Codes accounts, M3U playlists as a link or a file, Jellyfin and Emby servers, and XMLTV guides. From Jellyfin and Emby you get films and series for now. Live TV from those servers isn't there yet

Which video formats does it play?

HLS and MP4 play in Apple's own player, with Picture in Picture and AirPlay. MKV, AVI, raw MPEG-TS, WebM, RTMP and the rest go to Lumavu's second engine. You don't have to choose: it picks one, and tries the other if a stream won't play

How many channels can I watch at once?

Up to four in Multiview, which is part of Pro. Your provider decides how many streams an account can have at once, usually one or two. Lumavu knows the limit, and plays from another playlist when one account is full

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes, and it's the same purchase as on iPhone. Closed, the outside screen has the iPhone app. Open it and the inside screen switches to the iPad layout, with your playlists down the side

Is there Chromecast?

Not at launch. AirPlay works from iPhone, iPad and Mac, for streams that play in Apple's player
